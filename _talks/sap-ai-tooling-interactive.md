For this SAP AI tooling presentation, I wanted the audience to experience the idea through an activity. We used **helium balloons and a ladder** to make the relationship between people, their goals, and AI tangible.

The balloons represented AI achievements and were placed high enough that reaching them by jumping was difficult. Bringing in a ladder changed how the participants could approach the same task.

## AI as a way to reach further

The analogy was simple: **AI is the ladder**. The goal still matters, and people still take part in the work. The tools help them reach an outcome with less unnecessary effort.

The activity made room for participation and conversation about where AI can help in everyday workflows. It also gave the presentation a shared reference point: everyone could connect the discussion back to something they had just seen or tried.
