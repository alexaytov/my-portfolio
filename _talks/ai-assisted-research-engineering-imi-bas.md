On **14 November 2026**, I gave a three-hour session at the **Institute of Mathematics and Informatics, Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (IMI-BAS)**, as part of the i-Metode doctoral school in Sofia.

My talk, **AI-assisted research engineering: from coding assistance to reliable autonomous workflows**, focused on the use of AI in research engineering. It addressed the progression from help with programming to autonomous workflows, with reliability as a central concern.

The official programme lists the session in Bulgarian as <span lang="bg">"Изследователско инженерство с помощта на ИИ: от подпомагане на програмирането до надеждни автономни работни процеси"</span>. It took place from **14:00 to 17:00** in **Hall 055, "Acad. Stefan Dodunekov"**.

## About the doctoral school

The school ran from **13 to 15 November 2026**, bringing together researchers and industry experts to introduce doctoral students to current research methods in applied mathematics and informatics.

The first day focused on **research skills in a digital environment**. The following days covered computational approaches to data, AI-assisted research engineering, Abelian sandpiles and tropical geometry, and AI applications in the Internet of Things (AIoT).

IMI-BAS organized the school under the **i-Metode project** (BG05SFPR001-3.004-0012), which develops methods, technologies, and models to support a data-based economy. The project is funded through the **Education 2021-2027 programme**, co-financed by the **European Union**.
