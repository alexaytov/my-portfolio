I contributed a **Model Context Protocol (MCP) deep dive and live demonstration** to the launch of the local AI community at SAP Labs Bulgaria.

The session introduced MCP as a common way to connect AI applications with tools and information sources. The demonstration made that integration concrete, showing how the protocol can support practical interactions between an AI assistant and a connected system.

## Sharing practical AI experience

The launch brought colleagues together to exchange ideas about AI and its applications. My contribution focused on making a technical integration concept approachable and giving the audience a practical example to discuss.

The community was launched by **Mayada Khatib and Velizar Stoyanov**. Mayada's [event recap](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7379799368532525056/) documents the session and includes the photos shown here.
