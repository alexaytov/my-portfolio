## Three days in Sofia

I joined the Platform & AI Sofia Code Camp as **one of eight AI Experts** supporting colleagues as they worked on real agentic scenarios. This was **my third time in that role at a code camp**, after [Munich]({{ '/projects/sap-ai-code-camp-munich-scm/' | relative_url }}) and [Walldorf]({{ '/projects/sap-ai-code-camp-walldorf-private-cloud/' | relative_url }}).

Over three days, 110 participants from across SAP's Business AI Platform organization worked on 16 challenges. It was the **second AI Code Camp for Bulgaria**. The **seven teams** that made the most progress presented their solutions to an expert jury, and the **top three received awards**.

My part was to **help colleagues work through their questions** and share what I knew while they built. Having time together to try things is a big part of why I enjoy these camps.

<section class="code-camp-reflection" aria-labelledby="ai-expert-reflection" markdown="1">

## What being an AI Expert means to me
{: #ai-expert-reflection }

For me, much of being an AI Expert nowadays comes down to **staying curious**. There is always more to learn, and I want to keep making time to **play with ideas, try things out, and see what happens**. That experimentation gives me something useful to bring to the next conversation with a colleague.

The title on the shirt is nice. What I care about is **being useful to the person asking for help**, sharing what I've learned and being willing to work through a question together when I don't have the answer.
