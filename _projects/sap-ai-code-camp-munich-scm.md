## Overview

I participated as an **AI Expert** in the **SAP SCM AI Code Camp in Munich, Germany, from 8–10 July 2026**. The three-day camp brought together teams working in **Supply Chain Management (SCM)** to explore how AI could address business challenges and turn ideas into working prototypes.

The event combined hands-on development with expert coaching. Its focus was on applying AI to a concrete problem, building something that could be demonstrated, and learning through implementation.

## My Role as an AI Expert

My role was to coach and guide teams as they developed their ideas into practical AI solutions. The expert role covered:

- Helping teams connect their business problem with a focused AI use case and a manageable prototype.
- Providing technical guidance during development and helping teams work through implementation questions.
- Supporting clear, well-structured demonstrations of what a prototype could do.
- Exchanging guidance with fellow experts and connecting teams facing similar challenges.

## Bringing AI into a Business Domain

The SCM focus connected AI experimentation with the context of supply chain software. The camp gave teams time to explore an idea through code, discuss their approach with experts, and refine it as they built.

For me, this was an opportunity to contribute my AI integration experience in a coaching role, working alongside teams with their own domain knowledge. It brought together two areas of my work: applying AI in real systems and helping other developers build their skills.

I later participated as an AI Expert in the [SAP Private Cloud AI Code Camp in Walldorf]({{ '/projects/sap-ai-code-camp-walldorf-private-cloud/' | relative_url }}).
