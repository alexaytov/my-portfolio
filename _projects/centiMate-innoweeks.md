## Overview

Participated in SAP InnoWeeks 2024, an internal innovation challenge where teams develop creative solutions to real business problems. Our team built CentiMate, an innovative solution focused on improving developer productivity.

## Challenge

InnoWeeks challenges teams to:
- Identify real business pain points
- Develop working prototypes in 2 weeks
- Present solutions to judges and stakeholders
- Collaborate across departments and locations

## Our Solution

CentiMate addresses developer productivity by providing intelligent automation suggestions based on workflow patterns and common tasks.

## Key Learnings

- Rapid prototyping techniques
- Balancing innovation with practicality
- Effective team collaboration under time pressure
- Pitching technical solutions to diverse audiences
