## Overview

I participated as an **AI Expert** in the **SAP Private Cloud AI Code Camp in Walldorf, Germany, from 27–29 October 2026**. The event brought Private Cloud teams together for three days of practical AI development, supported by mentors and coaches.

The emphasis was on moving from an idea to a prototype: defining a useful problem, building and validating an approach, and demonstrating the result.

## My Role as an AI Expert

I supported the camp in an expert coaching role, helping teams approach AI integration and work through questions during development. The role centered on practical guidance: discussing implementation approaches, helping teams keep their prototypes focused, and sharing knowledge across teams and fellow experts.

This continued my involvement in SAP's AI Code Camps after the [SCM camp in Munich]({{ '/projects/sap-ai-code-camp-munich-scm/' | relative_url }}), this time in the context of Private Cloud software.

## From Idea to Demonstration

The camp connected learning with implementation. Teams could explore AI through their own development work and use feedback to refine what they were building.

## Focus of My Contribution

My contribution was technical coaching and knowledge sharing in a setting where teams were actively building. It extended my work on AI integration into real applications, with a focus on helping developers turn an initial idea into something concrete enough to test and discuss.
