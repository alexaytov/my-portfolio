## Overview

Participated as a mentor in the pilot edition of SAP's ITVersity program, an initiative designed to connect experienced SAP professionals with university students pursuing careers in technology.

## Program Description

ITVersity is SAP's mentorship program that bridges the gap between academic education and industry experience. The pilot program established the foundation for connecting mentors with mentees to provide guidance, knowledge sharing, and career development support.

## Mentor Responsibilities

- Provided guidance on career paths in software development
- Shared industry insights and best practices
- Helped mentees navigate technical and professional challenges
- Offered feedback on projects and skill development

## Impact

The pilot program successfully demonstrated the value of industry-academia collaboration, leading to the continuation and expansion of the ITVersity program.
