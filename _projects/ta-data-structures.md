## Overview

Served as a teaching assistant for the Data Structures and Programming course in the Computer Science program at the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics, Sofia University, during the Winter Semester 2025/2026.

## Course Website

Created a dedicated website with lectures, exercises, and presentations for the course:
[fmi-sdp-exc-2025-26](https://alexaytov.github.io/fmi-sdp-exc-2025-26/)

## Responsibilities

- **Lab Sessions**: Conducted weekly practical sessions helping students implement data structures
- **Code Review**: Reviewed and provided feedback on student assignments
- **Office Hours**: Held office hours to assist students with coursework and concepts
- **Grading**: Evaluated student submissions and provided constructive feedback

## Topics Covered

- Linked Lists (singly, doubly, circular)
- Stacks and Queues
- Trees (binary trees, BST, balanced trees)
- Heaps and Priority Queues
- Hash Tables
- Graphs and Graph Algorithms
- Sorting and Searching Algorithms
- Algorithm Complexity Analysis
