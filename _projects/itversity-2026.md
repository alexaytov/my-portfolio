## Overview

I am actively participating in the 2026 edition of SAP's ITVersity mentorship program as AI Stream Lead, while mentoring two mentees on projects that integrate AI into real applications. This continues my involvement from the pilot edition.

## Program Description

ITVersity connects SAP professionals with university students, providing mentorship, career guidance, and real-world industry insights. The 2026 program expands on lessons learned from the successful pilot edition.

## AI Stream Lead and Mentor

- Lead the AI stream within the program
- Mentor two mentees on AI integration into real application projects
- Provide ongoing mentorship and career guidance
- Knowledge sharing on software development practices
- Support with technical skill development
- Professional networking and industry exposure

## Program Opening

On 20 October 2026, I spoke at the SAP Labs Mentorship Program opening as one of the program's success stories. My message was **“Be the mentor you wish you had.”**

## Program Goals

- Foster talent development in the technology sector
- Create meaningful connections between industry and academia
- Prepare students for successful careers in software development
- Build a community of future technology leaders
