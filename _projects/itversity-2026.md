## Overview

Continuing as a mentor in the 2026 edition of SAP's ITVersity program, building on the success of the pilot program to guide the next generation of technology professionals.

## Program Description

ITVersity connects SAP professionals with university students, providing mentorship, career guidance, and real-world industry insights. The 2026 program expands on lessons learned from the successful pilot edition.

## Mentor Responsibilities

- Ongoing mentorship and career guidance for assigned mentees
- Knowledge sharing on software development practices
- Support with technical skill development
- Professional networking and industry exposure

## Program Goals

- Foster talent development in the technology sector
- Create meaningful connections between industry and academia
- Prepare students for successful careers in software development
- Build a community of future technology leaders
