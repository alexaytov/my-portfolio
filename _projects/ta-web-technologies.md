Since February 2026, I have been an **Honorary Teaching Assistant at Sofia University St. Kliment Ohridski**, leading the Web Technologies practicum for third-year students.

The course connects the foundations of web development with practical work across the browser, backend, and supporting infrastructure. My contribution combines teaching with creating materials that students can revisit outside the classroom.

## A home for course materials

I developed the [Web Technologies course website](https://alexaytov.github.io/fmi-web-tech/) to bring lectures, exercises, and presentations together in one place. The materials are in Bulgarian and pair explanations with code examples and practical tasks.

The published materials cover:

- **Web foundations:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, and the document object model.
- **Application development:** three-tier architecture, React, Angular, REST APIs, and databases.
- **Working with web systems:** sessions, security, Git, cloud services, and cloud-native development.

Students can move between a topic's lecture, presentation, and exercises as they work through the course. The [source repository](https://github.com/alexaytov/fmi-web-tech) is public.

## From the practicum to the lecture hall

As part of this teaching work, I delivered my first official university lecture, focusing on **REST**. It brought a topic from my software engineering work into the classroom for third-year students.

The [REST lecture page]({{ '/talks/rest-sofia-university/' | relative_url }}) includes the classroom photo, slides, and related exercises.
